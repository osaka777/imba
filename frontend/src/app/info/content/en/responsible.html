Responsible Gaming

Responsible gaming is an important part of our Company's customer service policy, which is why we take very seriously the issues that may arise as a result of gambling addiction. We consider it our direct duty to protect players from excessive gambling and to prevent persons under 21 years of age (and persons who have not reached the Permitted Age under the law of their jurisdiction) from participating in gambling. 

We want our service to be as convenient and functional as possible so that it is well suited for enjoyable leisure. Unfortunately, for some players, gambling can turn from an exciting hobby into a problem.

We fully support and share the responsible gambling policy used worldwide, and we do everything we can so that our Clients can enjoy a safe and exciting game without losing control. 

Staying in Control

Gambling at a bookmaker — is simply entertainment, a good way to spend time pleasantly, enjoy watching your favorite team, and find like-minded people among other bettors. While enjoying watching matches and gambling, it is nevertheless worth remembering that moderation is necessary. 

Every bettor should always remember:

gambling — is only a form of leisure, not a way to earn money; do not lose your sense of moderation:

  • if you lose, do not try to win back immediately, because there is always a chance to win next time; 
  • start playing on the condition that you can spend only the amount you have and no more;
  • carefully monitor the time and amount of money you spend on gambling.

Warning Signs of Gambling Addiction .

Among most people for whom gambling is merely an exciting way to spend time, there is a small percentage of players who suffer from gambling addiction. The results of recent studies have shown that only a small part of the adult population faces such a problem as gambling addiction. However, our Company treats this issue with the utmost seriousness and advises bettors to always remember that: 

  • gambling is based on the law of probability, so there are no "formulas" or "systems" that guarantee winnings; 
  • the desire to play should come exclusively from you; 
  • gambling — is entertainment, not a way to get rich quickly or pay off debts;
  • regularly monitor how much money you spend on gambling; 
  • to play, you must always know the rules of the game.

It is difficult to determine the line that separates healthy excitement from harmful addiction. However, there are certain signs that may indicate that a bettor is beginning to have problems.

You are invited to answer 10 questions. If you answered yes to at least 5 of them, there is a high probability that you already suffer from gambling addiction.

  1. Are you seriously involved in gambling?
  2. Are the amounts of your bets constantly increasing?
  3. Do you borrow money to gamble?
  4. Do you often play longer than you planned?
  5. Does frequent visits to the bookmaker negatively affect your reputation?
  6. Do you feel irritation or disappointment if you cannot place a bet?
  7. Is gambling a way for you to escape from problems? 
  8. Do you often have to win back losses?
  9. Have you tried to control the size of your bets or the time spent at the gambling venue, but your attempts were unsuccessful?
  10. Do you not tell your loved ones about your gambling hobby?

Tips for Managing Your Play

  1. Decide in advance how much time you will spend playing at the bookmaker;
  2. Set a maximum amount you are willing to lose and do not exceed it; Never borrow money to gamble;
  3. Try to find a new hobby and combine it with gambling;
  4. Never visit gambling venues if you are in a bad mood, depressed, or feeling down. 

Self-Exclusion from Gambling

If you wish to close your imba account, please contact customer support. In most cases, you will be able to restore your account later. However, if you request permanent closure, we will grant this request under any circumstances and account restoration will not be possible. The Company may also independently restrict or close an Account if there are signs of problem gambling, suspicion of participation by a person under 21 years of age, or on other grounds provided for in the Rules, without any obligation to compensate the Client for losses from bets.
